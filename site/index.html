Copy what's new, skip what you have

Pulli knows what you copied last time and what is already on your Mac - even under another name - so one click brings over only new photos. Every file is checked as it's written, with speed and time left in plain view.

See what fills your phone

A storage map shows where the space goes at a glance, and Pulli finds the photos and videos hiding in WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and Viber.

Free up space, safely

After a copy, Pulli can delete the originals from the phone - but only after checking each copy on your Mac against the phone, byte for byte.

Fits in on your Mac

A menu bar item and desktop widgets show progress at a glance and copy new photos in one click. Close the window and Pulli keeps working. There's a plug-in chime, notifications, Shortcuts actions, and a cable check that tells you when another cable or port would copy faster.

Built in: copies that finish

Pull the cable. Pulli carries on.

Big copies take a while, and over USB a lot can happen. Wherever a copy stops, Pulli continues from that exact byte - even halfway through a 4 GB video.

The cable is pulled

Waits for the same phone, then continues by itself when it's back - in any port.

The phone locks, or its USB mode changes

Tells you what to tap, and continues as soon as the phone allows it.

The Mac sleeps, or you close the lid

Keeps the Mac awake while copying. If it sleeps anyway, continues when it wakes.

Pulli is quit, crashes or is force-quit

Continues from the last saved block the next time it runs.

The Mac restarts

Picks up the unfinished copy when Pulli opens again - at login, if you let it.

The disk fills up

Pauses with a clear message, and continues once there is room.

How it works

  1. Small, saved blocksPulli copies in 4 MB blocks. Each block is written to disk before it's recorded in Pulli's copy journal - so the journal never claims more than is really on your Mac.
  2. Nothing read twiceOn resume, Pulli checks the file on the phone hasn't changed, then continues from the last saved block.
  3. Never a half fileA file keeps a .part name until it's complete, and every file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint as it's copied.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Install Pulli

Download Pulli, open the disk image and drag Pulli to Applications. Or use Homebrew.

Plug in your phone

Use a USB cable that carries data, and unlock the phone.

Choose File transfer

On the phone, tap the USB notification and choose File transfer. Tap Allow if it asks.

Which Macs

Every Mac with Apple silicon - M1 or newer - running macOS 26 Tahoe or later. Intel Macs are not supported. To check: Apple menu › About This Mac shows “Chip: Apple M…”.

Which phones

Any Android phone with a USB cable that carries data and offers “File transfer”. Tested with Samsung Galaxy and Lenovo phones.
