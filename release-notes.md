The first public release of Pulli: copy photos and files from your Android phone to your Mac, and never lose progress.

- **Copies that finish.** A pulled cable, a locked phone, a sleeping Mac or a crash pauses the copy; it continues from the exact byte where it stopped, and every file is checked.
- **Only what is new.** Copy new photos in one click, and skip anything already on your Mac, even under another name.
- **Your phone in Finder.** The phone appears under Locations in Finder's sidebar; copy and paste or drag files both ways.
- **Free up space safely.** Delete originals from the phone only after each copy is checked against it.
- **Storage map**, messaging-app media, a menu bar item, desktop widgets, notifications, Shortcuts actions and a cable check.
- **Automatic updates** and **Send Feedback** in the Help menu.
- **Privacy:** Clear History forgets past copies, the activity log and saved previews.
